Introduction
1.1Sheffield City Council is undertaking market engagement to understand the availability, capability, implementation approach and indicative pricing models for cloud-based Customer Relationship Management (CRM) solutions.
1.2The responses received will help us shape our requirements and determine the strategy we will take to achieve our desired outcome.  
1.3 This engagement is for information-gathering only and does not constitute a call for competition, a commitment to procure, or an invitation to tender. Any subsequent procurement will be undertaken in accordance with the applicable procurement route and buying process.

Project Details and High-Level Requirements
The Council is undertaking preliminary market engagement to understand the current market for cloud-based CRM solutions suitable for a local authority environment. The Council is considering options for a solution that supports improved customer contact, digital service delivery and case management across council services. The solution will operate as the multi-channel front-door for access to council services.
The Councilâ€™s existing CRM platform is a large-scale, business-critical solution that is embedded across multiple services and used by a significant number of staff and customers. The platform supports approximately a million customer interactions per year, is accessed by around 3000 users across 300 service areas, and underpins the delivery of over 500 online forms, workflows and customer processes.
We are particularly interested in solutions including but not limited to:
Core CRM and customer account functionality
Omnichannel customer contact management (telephone, web, email, webchat, social media and AI-assisted channels)
End-to-end case management and workflow capabilities
Citizen self-service/citizen portal and digital forms
Integration with Microsoft 365, Power Platform, Azure and common local government back-office applications
AI, automation and Copilot-enabled capabilities
Knowledge management
Reporting, analytics and performance management 
Implementation approaches, timescales and indicative commercial models
Experience of delivery within UK local authorities and other public sector organisations.
The council are interested in gathering information from suppliers who are able to meet prescribed criteria such as:
Cyber Essentials Plus and preferably ISO 27001 accredited, with robust security management processes
Secure user access through Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) integration, Single Sign-On (SSO) and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
Encryption of all council data at rest and in transit, with data hosted in approved UK or compliant jurisdictions 
Comprehensive audit logging, monitoring and incident management, including timely notification of security breaches 
Proven business continuity and disaster recovery arrangements, with defined recovery objectives and regular resilience testing.
This engagement is for information-gathering purposes only and to help the Council understand the market and shape its requirement and future strategy regarding the provision of a CRM system.  It does not constitute a procurement, call for competition, invitation to tender or commitment to award a contract. Any future procurement will be carried out in accordance with the applicable procurement rules and the relevant framework buying process.
